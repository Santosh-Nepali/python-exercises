  1️⃣  TAKE
      Only works at the Collection Point — picks up a random
      waste item across Bio / Paper / Energy / Plastic / Mixed
      Waste, and adds it to your inventory.

  2️⃣  MOVE
      Move between the Collection Point and the 5 waste bins.

  3️⃣  DROP
      Sort a carried item into whichever bin you're standing in.
      ✅ Correct bin → +1 point
      ❌ Wrong bin   → −1 point, with an explanation

  4️⃣  INVENTORY
      Shows the waste items you're currently carrying.

  5️⃣  SCORE
      Shows your current score.

  6️⃣  USER-PROFILE
      Displays registered users' details.

  7️⃣  HELP
      Explains how to play the game.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  💡  Tip: Sort fast, sort true — accuracy matters more than speed!
